About Vo Phu Thinh

Vo Phu Thinh (Võ Phú Thịnh) is an AI Engineer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. He works at the AI Center of Excellence of HPT Vietnam Corporation, where he designs, builds and deploys production AI solutions built on Large Language Models (LLM), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents and classical Machine Learning.

In his first year as an AI Engineer he shipped three AI products to production: an event chatbot that answered more than 850 attendee questions at HPT D-DAY 2025, Lyly, an enterprise AI assistant with real-time voice and text, hybrid-search RAG and multi-step reasoning, and H.I.H HotNews Intelligence Hub, which automates business intelligence for most HPT employees. Lyly and H.I.H won second and third prize in the HPT AI in Business competition in 2025.

His daily toolbox includes Python, PyTorch, LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, OpenAI and Azure OpenAI models, Azure AI Search, Qdrant, FastAPI, Docker and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). He studied Information Technology at HUTECH and Open University Malaysia (2021–2025) and works in Vietnamese and English.

He is open to AI Engineer roles (full-time, contract and remote) and can deliver a production-ready chatbot or RAG system within six weeks. See the portfolio for project details, the contact page to get in touch, or the API documentation for machine-readable access to this information.
